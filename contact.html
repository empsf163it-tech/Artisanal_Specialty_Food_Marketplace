Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, shipping, and maker applications.

How are fragile glass bottles & sourdough shipped safely? ▾
We package all liquid & fragile items in custom-cut 100% biodegradable molded pulp shells that cushion against high impact transit.
Can I schedule a specific delivery date for corporate gift hampers? ▾
Yes, our corporate concierge allows you to select precise arrival windows up to 90 days in advance.
How do you verify maker authenticity and food safety? ▾
Our curation committee conducts on-site farm visits, soil audits, and micro-batch chemical purity testing before onboarding any maker.
Are all products 100% free of artificial preservatives? ▾
Yes. We strictly prohibit artificial colorings, synthetic stabilizers, and chemical preservatives across the entire marketplace.
